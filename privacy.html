PRIVACY POLICY

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 6, 2026

Welcome to MIND BEND (https://mind-bend-five.vercel.app/). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting any information when you play our fullscreen optical illusion game on web or mobile devices.

1. Information We Collect

MIND BEND is designed as a privacy-first game. We do NOT collect personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

  • Game Data: Local high scores, highest tap counts, and unlocked optical illusion themes are stored locally on your device via browser localStorage or Android SharedPreferences.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use third-party tracking scripts, cookies, or telemetry to monitor individual users.

2. How We Use Data

Since all high score data remains stored locally on your own device, it is strictly used to display your game progress and high score records in the game HUD.

3. Third-Party Services

MIND BEND does not share any data with third parties or advertising networks.

4. Children's Privacy

MIND BEND does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, nor do we collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out via GitHub at Victorezhil310/MIND-BEND.
